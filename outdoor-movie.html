Questions

Good Things to Know

What do I need to provide?

Just a WiFi connection for the Chromecast. We bring everything else. Make sure you have an outdoor outlet or extension cord accessible near the setup area.

What happens at the end of the night?

When your movie ends, just unplug the power from the equipment table and bring it inside for the night. The screen stays staked in the yard and the speakers can stay outside — we'll collect everything in the morning.

What can I stream?

Anything available on your streaming accounts — Netflix, Disney+, Amazon Prime, YouTube, and more. You control what plays from your phone or device.

What if it rains?

We'll work with you to reschedule. Weather can be unpredictable — reach out as soon as you know and we'll find a new date that works.

How far in advance should I book?

As soon as you know your date — especially for weekends and summer/fall evenings, which fill up quickly. We recommend at least a week out, though we'll always do our best on short notice.

Do you serve areas outside Augusta?

Yes — we serve Columbia County, Evans, Grovetown, and surrounding areas within 25 miles. Farther out is possible with a travel fee. Just ask.
