Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

Augusta Media Solutions ("we," "us," or "our") provides TV mounting, cable management, AV equipment rental, and outdoor movie night services in Augusta, GA and the surrounding area. This page explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and who we share it with.

Information We Collect

When you request a quote, book a service, or contact us through this site, we collect the information you provide directly, which may include:

  • Your name, phone number, and email address
  • Your service address or general location
  • Details about your project (TV size, wall type, event date, equipment needed, etc.)
  • Photos you choose to upload of your space to help us prepare an accurate quote

We also use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use this site (pages viewed, general location, device type). This does not include the personal details you submit through a quote form.

How We Use Your Information

  • To prepare and send you a quote
  • To schedule and carry out the service you requested
  • To contact you about your request, appointment, or an order in progress
  • To understand and improve how people use our website

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for anything beyond providing and improving our services.

Who We Share It With

We use the following third-party services to operate this site and our business:

  • Formspree — processes and delivers the quote request forms on this site to us. Formspree's privacy policy
  • Google Analytics — provides anonymized site usage statistics. Google's privacy policy

We do not share your information with any other third party except as required by law.

Photos You Upload

If you upload photos of your TV, wall, or room through our quote form, we use them only to prepare an accurate estimate and plan the job. We don't publish, sell, or reuse your photos for marketing without asking you first.

Cookies

This site uses cookies set by Google Analytics to help us understand site traffic. You can disable cookies through your browser settings at any time; doing so won't affect your ability to request a quote or use the site.

Your Choices

You can ask us to delete the information you've submitted, or ask what information we have on file, by contacting us using the information below. We'll respond as quickly as we can.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.
