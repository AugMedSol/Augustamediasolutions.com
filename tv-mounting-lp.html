FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before booking.

How much does TV mounting cost? +
TV mounting starts at $150 for TVs 50" or under, and $250 for TVs 55"–86". Add-ons like cable concealment, outlet relocation, and soundbar mounting are available. Use our instant quote form to get an exact price for your setup.
Do you provide the mount? +
We can provide a tilting or full-motion mount as an add-on for $57.50. If you already have a mount, we're happy to install it — just select "No Mount Needed" on the quote form.
What wall types can you mount on? +
We mount on all wall types including drywall, brick, concrete, stone, tile, and wood panel. No wall is too challenging for us.
How long does installation take? +
A standard single TV mount takes 45–90 minutes. More complex installs with cable concealment or multiple TVs may take 2–3 hours.
Do you serve areas outside Augusta? +
Yes! We serve Augusta, GA and all surrounding areas. Submit a quote with your city and we'll confirm availability for your location.
