Pro Tips from Our Installers
The ideal viewing height centers the TV at eye level when seated — typically 42–57" from the floor depending on your couch height.
Avoid mounting too high — tilting your neck upward for long periods causes strain. Lower is almost always better than you think.
Full-motion mounts are great for corner walls or rooms where you watch from multiple seating positions.
For bedroom TVs, a slightly higher mount (55–65") is more comfortable when watching from bed.
